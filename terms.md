# MoneyHub Terms of Use

**Last updated: 14 October 2026**

## 1. About MoneyHub

MoneyHub is a personal finance desktop application for Windows. It is designed to help users organise and review their financial information.

MoneyHub is currently under development. Features may change, be incomplete or become unavailable.

## 2. Personal finance information

MoneyHub provides tools for recording and displaying financial information. It does not provide regulated financial advice, investment recommendations or guarantees about financial outcomes.

You should independently verify important financial information against your bank or other authoritative records.

## 3. Open Banking

MoneyHub is being developed to support read-only bank account connections through Enable Banking.

Bank connections will require your authorisation and may be subject to availability, consent expiry, bank restrictions and third-party service requirements.

MoneyHub is not intended to initiate payments or transfer funds.

## 4. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for protecting your computer, account credentials, encryption passwords and backups.

You should keep independent backups of important financial records and verify the accuracy of imported or synchronised information.

## 5. Availability and accuracy

MoneyHub may contain errors or experience interruptions. Account balances and transactions obtained through banking services may be delayed or incomplete.

Do not rely solely on MoneyHub when making time-sensitive financial decisions.

## 6. Third-party services

Banking connections may depend on Enable Banking and participating banks. Their separate terms and privacy notices may apply.
