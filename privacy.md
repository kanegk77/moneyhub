# MoneyHub Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 14 October 2026**

## 1. About MoneyHub

MoneyHub is a Windows desktop application designed to help individuals manage their personal finances, including bank accounts, transactions, budgets and investments.

**Contact:** [YOUR PUBLIC CONTACT EMAIL]

## 2. Information MoneyHub processes

Depending on the features you choose to use, MoneyHub may process:

- Bank account names, identifiers and balances
- Transaction dates, descriptions and amounts
- Budget and investment information
- Financial records you enter or import
- Technical information needed to establish and maintain banking connections

## 3. Open Banking

MoneyHub is being developed to support read-only Open Banking connections through Enable Banking.

When this feature becomes available, you will be asked to authorise access through your bank's consent process.

MoneyHub is not intended to initiate payments or transfer money.

Access to banking information will depend on your bank's permissions, the consent you provide and the capabilities of Enable Banking.

## 4. Storage and security

MoneyHub is designed to store financial records locally in an encrypted vault on your computer.

MoneyHub also offers optional encrypted synchronisation with a server configured by the user.

Local storage and encryption do not eliminate every security risk. You are responsible for protecting access to your computer, backups and any synchronisation server you operate.

## 5. Third-party services

If you choose to connect a bank account, Enable Banking and your bank may process information needed to establish and operate that connection.

Their own privacy notices and terms may also apply.

## 6. Your choices

You can choose whether to use optional banking connections and synchronisation features.

You can manage financial records within MoneyHub and stop using the application. Removing local records does not necessarily delete backups, synchronised copies or information retained by third-party banking services.

## 7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as MoneyHub develops, particularly when live Open Banking functionality becomes available.
